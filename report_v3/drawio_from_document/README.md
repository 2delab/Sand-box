How they are built: each box, group and arrow was read from the Mermaid source of the document's figure, so positions, sizes and routing match the picture. Groups are containers, so moving a group moves its boxes. Arrows stay attached to their boxes when you move them. Colours follow the document (default Mermaid theme, with the red bottleneck and dashed inferred styles).

To edit: open at app.diagrams.net (File → Open from device). Double-click any box or arrow label to change its text.

Note: the arrows keep the routing from the original figure. Rearranging boxes may leave bends that you can tidy with the Arrange → Waypoints tools.
